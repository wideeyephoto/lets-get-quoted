# Sparky AI Sidekick & SMS Messaging Policy

1. **Never include "Sparky" in SMS / Text Messages**:
   - The name "Sparky" must **NEVER** appear inside any SMS message body, MMS text, carrier confirmation receipt, or client-facing text link.
   - Outbound SMS messages sent over carrier networks must use clean, professional, transactional formatting (e.g. `[LGQ] J-104 (Miller): Added $450.00 material cost.` or `[CompanyName] J-104: Added $450.00 line item.`).
   - Every SMS text must strictly adhere to the GSM 7-bit ASCII basic character set (no emojis, fancy quotes, or em-dashes that inflate carrier segments).

2. **"Sparky" is an Internal Conceptual Assistant Persona**:
   - Sparky is solely a friendly concept and UI mascot on marketing pages and in the dashboard to give contractors a tangible assistant to address ("Text Sparky from the truck").
   - Phone contact cards (`.vcf`) must be generated cleanly as `[Business Name] Field Hotline` so they remain professional in phone contacts and phonebooks.

3. **100% White-Labeled for Homeowners & Clients**:
   - Homeowners and external customers must never see or hear the name "Sparky" or "Let's Get Quoted". All client-facing communications, portals, quotes, invoices, and reminders are branded 100% to the contractor's business.
