# Purpose
Export client, invoice, and job CSV reports, and connect QuickBooks or calendar sync tools.

# Steps
## Export complete workspace records
1. Go to Settings → Integrations & Export.
2. Select the dataset to export: Clients, Invoices, Payments, Jobs, or Timecards.
3. Choose your date range and click "Download CSV".

## Connect accounting integrations
Connect QuickBooks Online to automatically sync paid invoices, customers, and payment fee records.



# Done State
You will keep your accounting and CRM records synced with full data portability.

# Common Failure
**QuickBooks sync shows an authentication error.**
Open Settings → Integrations, disconnect the QuickBooks connection, and reconnect to refresh OAuth tokens.
