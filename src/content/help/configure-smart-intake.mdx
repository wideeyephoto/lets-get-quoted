# Purpose
Customize website intake questions, emergency tags, photo uploads, and instant lead scoring.

# Steps
## Customize trade intake questions
1. Select trade-specific intake fields (e.g. pipe material for plumbing, unit age for HVAC).
2. Enable photo and video uploads so homeowners submit pictures of their issue.
3. Enable instant emergency triage questions (e.g. active water leaks, sparking panel).

## Embed on external websites or use standalone links
Copy the Smart Intake iframe code to embed on WordPress, Squarespace, or Wix sites, or share your direct intake link in local ads.



# Done State
You will collect structured project requirements and photos on every incoming homeowner request.

# Common Failure
**Intake form submissions do not notify the owner.**
Verify Owner Alert Phone is configured under Settings → Business.
