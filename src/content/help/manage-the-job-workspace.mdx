# Purpose
Track live project execution, crew assignments, milestone checklists, and job cost accounting.

# Steps
## Monitor live project progress
1. Open any active Job to view customer contact info, job site address, scope notes, and milestone checklists.
2. Review field technician time entries and material receipts logged from the road.
3. Generate change orders or progress invoices as project phases complete.

## Job deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
WARNING: Deleting a job cannot be undone. Deleting a job hard-deletes the job file, milestone history, scheduled crew appointments, and all logged job costs and material records. If you only want to close out work, mark the job as Completed or Cancelled instead.



# Done State
You will maintain complete operational control and audit trails across every active project.

# Common Failure
**A job was accidentally deleted.**
Job deletion cannot be undone. Linked historical Stripe payment records remain in Payments for accounting, but the job file must be re-created if needed.
