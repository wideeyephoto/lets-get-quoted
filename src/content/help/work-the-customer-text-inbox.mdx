# Purpose
Manage two-way SMS conversations, photo messages, and understand the carrier STOP opt-out invariant.

# Steps
## Communicate in real time from one business thread
1. Open Messages to view customer conversations unified across web intake, quote comments, and direct SMS.
2. Send text replies, estimate links, payment receipts, or site photos in seconds.
3. Assign conversations to specific office team members for follow-up.

## The STOP opt-out trap: Save button will NOT re-opt
CRITICAL CARRIER INVARIANT: When a customer replies STOP, carrier-level automation immediately marks their phone number as opted out. Clicking "Save" or editing their client card in the dashboard will NEVER re-opt them. To resume text messages, the customer MUST text START or UNSTOP from their own physical mobile handset.

## Block contact is permanent
Clicking "Block Contact" stops all incoming messages permanently. There is no dashboard unblock action, so reserve blocking exclusively for severe spam or harassment.



# Done State
You will maintain fast customer response times while remaining 100% compliant with carrier messaging rules.

# Common Failure
**Messages to a customer are failing with opt-out errors.**
The customer texted STOP. Ask them to text "START" or "UNSTOP" to your business number to re-enable messaging.
