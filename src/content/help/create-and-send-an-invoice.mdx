# Purpose
Generate final invoices from completed jobs, apply discounts and taxes, and send payment links.

# Steps
## Generate, print, or send an invoice from job scope
1. Click "Create Invoice" from the job file or Invoices workspace.
2. Scope items, materials, and approved change orders populate automatically with previous deposits deducted.
3. Apply sales tax percentage or promo discounts as needed.
4. Click "Send Invoice" to deliver the payment link via SMS and email.

## Track invoice status in real time
- Draft: Editable invoice being prepared.
- Sent: Delivered to customer; awaiting payment.
- Overdue: Past due date; automated reminder sequences activate.
- Paid: Funds collected and credited to job accounting.



# Done State
You will bill clients promptly and get paid faster with 1-tap mobile payment links.

# Common Failure
**Customer cannot open the invoice payment link.**
Resend the invoice link from the invoice action menu or copy the payment URL directly.
