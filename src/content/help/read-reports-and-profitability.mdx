# Purpose
Analyze project gross margins, revenue by trade service, and crew labor efficiency.

# Steps
## Analyze job profitability and gross margin
1. Open Reports → Profitability to view gross profit (Billed Revenue minus Direct Labor and Materials).
2. Filter by trade category to compare plumbing, HVAC, electrical, or general contracting margins.
3. Inspect individual job scorecards to identify jobs that ran over labor hour budgets.

## Export financial reports
Export detailed CSV summaries of revenue, taxes collected, and labor allocations for your CPA or bookkeeping software.



# Done State
You will identify your most profitable services and price future jobs with confidence.

# Common Failure
**Job gross margin shows as 100%.**
Ensure crew timecards and material receipts were logged and attached to the job file.
