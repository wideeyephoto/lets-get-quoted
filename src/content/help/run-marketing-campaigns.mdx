# Purpose
Broadcast SMS and email promotions, understand immediate send execution, and the 250-recipient limit.

# Steps
## Compose your promotional campaign
1. Open Campaigns and click "Create Campaign".
2. Choose SMS Broadcast or Email Newsletter.
3. Write your message with a compelling call-to-action and direct booking link.
4. Preview the message on desktop and mobile mockups.

## Understand campaign send realities and limits
- Immediate Execution: Marketing sends are executed immediately upon clicking Send. There is no delayed queue or cancel-after-send mechanism.
- 250-Recipient Cap: Campaign audiences are sliced to MAX_RECIPIENTS = 250 per run. If your audience has 400 contacts, the system sends to the first 250.
- Opted-In List Composition: The SMS broadcast list filters on status = opted_in. Contacts generated during transactional quoting or booking are included unless they have texted STOP.
- Performance Tracking: Tracks actual operational metrics: runs, sent, queued, and failed counts.



# Done State
You will launch revenue-generating marketing broadcasts while staying within delivery and compliance limits.

# Common Failure
**Campaign sends to fewer people than expected.**
Campaign runs are capped at 250 recipients (MAX_RECIPIENTS = 250), and contacts who previously texted STOP are automatically excluded.

**Campaign cannot be sent.**
Verify a physical business mailing address is saved in Settings → Business (required for anti-spam compliance).
