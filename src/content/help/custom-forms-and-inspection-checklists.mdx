# Purpose
Create dynamic conditional forms for customer website intake and safety/pre-trip inspection checklists for crew trucks.

# Steps
## Design conditional customer intake questionnaires
1. Open Forms and click "Create New Form".
2. Add question types: text fields, photo uploads, multiple-choice dropdowns, and date pickers.
3. Configure conditional branching rules (e.g. "If project type is Roof Replacement, show roof slope and shingle type questions").

## Deploy field safety and vehicle inspection checklists
- Pre-Trip Vehicle Checks: Require technicians to check fluid levels, tire pressure, and ladder rack securement before morning roll-out.
- Jobsite Safety & Hazard Audits: Log electrical panel safety verifications or water shutoff valve locations before starting demo.
- Punch List Sign-Off: Technicians verify completed line items and attach inspection photos directly from the Field App.

## Embed forms on your website or share direct links
Embed your custom questionnaires on any contractor website page or text direct form links to homeowners before on-site estimate visits.



# Done State
You will capture exact project specifications upfront and enforce consistent jobsite quality control.

# Common Failure
**Conditional question rule does not trigger.**
Verify that the parent field option value in the Form Editor matches the trigger condition exactly.

**Field inspection checklist is not showing on active jobs.**
Ensure the checklist template is linked to the active job trade category in workflow settings.
