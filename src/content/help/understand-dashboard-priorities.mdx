# Purpose
Prioritize unread messages, urgent leads, today’s schedule, and overdue balances.

# Steps
## Work from the top down
1. Review the Action Required queue: pending quotes, unread client messages, and new leads waiting for response.
2. Check Today’s Schedule to confirm crew assignments and customer arrival windows.
3. Examine the Revenue & Cash tracker to review deposits collected and overdue invoice reminders.

## Use the recommendations, not just the totals
The dashboard highlights proactive recommendations—such as unquoted leads approaching 24 hours old or past-due invoices ready for automated follow-up.



# Done State
You will start each morning knowing exactly which customer-waiting items and revenue opportunities require action.

# Common Failure
**Metrics do not match expectations.**
Verify date range filters and check whether test records were archived or excluded.
