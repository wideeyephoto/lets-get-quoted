# Purpose
Capture before/after photos, append progress voice notes, and issue customer-approved change orders.

# Steps
## Log visual proof and voice memos from the field
1. Use the 🎙️ Voice Note button in the field job interface (/field/jobs/[id]) or text a voice memo to log site observations.
2. Take clear photos of hidden damage (e.g. rotted wood, unexpected wiring) directly into the job file.
3. Tag photos as "Internal" or "Client Visible".

## Create and send a change order
When extra work is needed, generate a Change Order from the job workspace. Specify additional materials and labor, and send an SMS approval link so the homeowner approves the price increase before work continues.



# Done State
You will protect profit margins on unexpected job site conditions with signed change orders and photo proof.

# Common Failure
**Homeowner claims they did not approve extra charges.**
Open the job file to view the timestamped change order record, digital signature, and photo attachments.
