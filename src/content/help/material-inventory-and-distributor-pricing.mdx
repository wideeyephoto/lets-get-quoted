# Purpose
Track stock on service trucks, connect wholesale distributor pricing (Ferguson, ABC Supply, Graybar), and automate material purchase orders.

# Steps
## Track warehouse and truck inventory
1. Navigate to Inventory and configure items stocked on primary service vehicles (e.g. fittings, wire rolls, breakers, refrigerant).
2. Set baseline minimum quantity reorder thresholds for each vehicle.
3. Technicians deduct items from truck stock when completing jobs or logging field materials.

## Connect distributor catalog pricing
- Live Wholesale Price Feeds: Link distributor supplier accounts (Ferguson, ABC Supply, Graybar, Johnstone) to pull live contractor tier discounts.
- Direct EDI 850 Transmission: Transmit electronic purchase orders directly to local supply house branches.
- Price Book Sync: Automatically update your material line items when distributor wholesale costs increase.

## Automate job material pick-lists and purchase orders
Approved quote line items instantly generate a job pick-list. Office managers can export a supplier purchase order with one click, saving technicians time at the supply counter.



# Done State
You will stop running out of critical parts in the field and quote accurate supply costs with live distributor price sheets.

# Common Failure
**Distributor catalog pricing shows outdated rates.**
Click "Refresh Price Catalog" in Inventory settings to sync the latest branch pricing feed.

**Truck stock counts do not match physical inventory.**
Perform a quick vehicle audit from the Field App and submit a count adjustment.
