# Purpose
Maintain customer contact details, service properties, equipment tags, and understand irreversible actions.

# Steps
## Maintain client profiles and property addresses
1. View complete communication history, quote drafts, past invoices, and warranty documents for each client.
2. Add multiple service property addresses under a single commercial or residential customer account.
3. Attach equipment tags, gate codes, and pet safety notes for field technicians.

## Irreversible client actions: Merge and Block
- Merge Clients: WARNING: Merging client records cannot be undone. Merging permanently deletes the duplicate client profiles and detaches historical warranty records. Ensure you designate the correct master record before confirming.
- Block Contact: WARNING: Blocking a contact cannot be undone in the dashboard (no unblock surface exists). Blocked numbers can never send incoming leads or texts.
- Revoke Portal Access: Owner-only action that immediately invalidates customer portal magic links.



# Done State
You will keep clean customer records and avoid accidental data loss when managing duplicate accounts.

# Common Failure
**An office user cannot see Merge or Delete buttons.**
Destructive client operations require Owner role authorization.

**Client profile merged with wrong record.**
Client merging cannot be undone. You will need to manually re-enter the separated contact details.
