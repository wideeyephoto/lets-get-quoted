# Purpose
Complete the minimum setup needed to receive a lead, send a quote, and collect a payment.

# Steps
## Complete the foundation first
1. Open Settings → Business to confirm your legal company name, public phone, and time zone.
2. Connect Stripe in Settings → Payments so payouts land in your checking account.
3. Add your first price book item or starter service so you can assemble an estimate in seconds.

## Run one safe test
1. Create a test lead using your own mobile number and email.
2. Build and send a sample quote to experience the homeowner view.
3. Review the live dashboard cards to verify the test lead and quote appear in your active pipeline.

## Use the onboarding checklist
The dashboard checklist tracks your progress across business profile setup, website publishing, Stripe connection, and texting activation.



# Done State
You will have a verified workspace ready to accept real customer requests and get paid without test-mode confusion.

# Common Failure
**Stripe onboarding is stuck or pending.**
Open Settings → Payments and check for missing identity or bank verification prompts from Stripe.

**Sample SMS does not arrive.**
Verify your phone number in Settings and check that carrier registration is active.
