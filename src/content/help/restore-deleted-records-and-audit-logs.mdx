# Purpose
Recover accidentally deleted leads, quotes, clients, and jobs from Trash within the retention grace period and inspect tamper-evident audit history.

# Steps
## Soft deletion lifecycle and 30-day Trash grace period
When you delete a lead, quote draft, customer profile, or job, the item is soft-deleted and staged in /dashboard/trash for 30 days. Soft deletion prevents accidental loss from team misclicks while immediately removing items from active queues.

## Restore records with intact historical relationships
1. Open Trash from the sidebar or Settings.
2. Filter by record type: Leads, Quotes, Jobs, or Clients.
3. Click "Restore Record" on the desired item. The record instantly reappears in its original pipeline stage with all associated photo attachments, timecards, and message histories preserved.

## Inspect tamper-evident Activity and Audit Logs
Open the Activity workspace to review chronological audit logs. Every creation, update, stage transition, payment collection, and deletion event records the initiating user email, timestamp, IP context, and change details for forensic compliance.



# Done State
You will protect your business from accidental data loss and maintain a complete compliance record of who changed or deleted what.

# Common Failure
**A deleted record is not visible in Trash.**
Items past the 30-day retention grace period are permanently purged according to security compliance retention schedules.

**Non-owner user cannot access Trash restore buttons.**
Restoring sensitive client or financial records requires elevated Office or Owner clearance.
