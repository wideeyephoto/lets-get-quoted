# Purpose
Offer fixed-price diagnostic visits and quick service repairs for same-day schedule filling.

# Steps
## Set up fixed-price diagnostic options
1. Define Quick Stop services (e.g. "$99 AC Diagnostic Check", "$125 Drain Inspection").
2. Set maximum job duration (e.g. 45–60 minutes).
3. Require upfront card authorization upon booking to prevent no-shows.

## Route into open technician gaps
Quick Stops allow dispatchers to slot minor repairs into mid-day schedule openings without disrupting major jobs.



# Done State
You will fill calendar gaps between major projects with high-margin quick repair calls.

# Common Failure
**A Quick Stop required major unexpected repairs.**
Convert the Quick Stop into a full Job and issue a Change Order for additional scope.
