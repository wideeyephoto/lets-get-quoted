# Purpose
Forecast 30-day incoming cash flow, monitor payout dates, and predict upcoming expenses.

# Steps
## Understand the cash flow forecast model
1. Open Reports → Cash Flow to inspect projected 30-day inflows and outflows.
2. Inflows project confirmed deposits, active stage invoices, and scheduled job completions.
3. Outflows project crew labor costs and supplier material expenditures.

## Monitor payout transit timing
The transit pipeline displays funds currently in flight from Stripe to your bank account, highlighting exact expected deposit dates.



# Done State
You will anticipate cash needs and avoid cash flow pinches on materials and payroll.

# Common Failure
**A completed job does not show in cash flow inflows.**
Verify that an invoice has been generated and sent for the completed job.
