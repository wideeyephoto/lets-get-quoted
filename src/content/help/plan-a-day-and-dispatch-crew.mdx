# Purpose
Optimize daily drive routes, assign lead technicians, and publish daily schedules.

# Steps
## Organize daily technician routes
1. Open Schedule in Day view to inspect overlapping appointments and geographic clusters.
2. Drag and drop jobs to balance workload across available crew leads.
3. Review travel times and buffer windows between job sites.

## Dispatch and notify field workers
Publishing the schedule syncs assigned jobs directly to the crew field portal at /field, giving technicians one-tap driving directions and customer access instructions.



# Done State
Your crews will receive clear daily routes and homeowners will know when to expect technicians.

# Common Failure
**A technician cannot see today’s dispatched job.**
Verify the technician is assigned as an active crew member on the job and that their field app is synced.
