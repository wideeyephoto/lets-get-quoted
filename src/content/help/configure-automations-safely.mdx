# Purpose
Set up automated lead responses, review requests, quiet hours, and avoid spam filtering.

# Steps
## Understand the core automation rules
1. Open Automations to review available event triggers: New Lead, Quote Sent, Job Completed, and Invoice Paid.
2. Set automated delay timers (e.g. send review request 2 hours after job completion).
3. Enforce Quiet Hours (e.g. 8:00 PM to 8:00 AM in the recipient's or account's local time zone) so automated texts are paused until morning.

## Test automations before enabling
Always test automation sequences on your own phone number before enabling them for all live customer traffic.



# Done State
Your automations will nurture leads and follow up with clients without annoying customers or violating carrier rules.

# Common Failure
**Automated text was delayed until morning.**
Quiet Hours settings prevent automated SMS delivery during nighttime hours (evaluated in the recipient's or account's local time zone). Messages queue and send automatically when quiet hours end.
