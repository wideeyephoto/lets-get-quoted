# Purpose
Add field technicians, invite crew via magic link, assign labor rates, and control field permissions.

# Steps
## Add crew members and send magic link invitations
1. Open Crew → Team and click "Add Crew Member".
2. Enter their full name, mobile number, email address, and hourly labor pay rate.
3. Send the invitation: crew access is delivered via an emailed magic link (not an SMS invite).
4. The technician clicks their magic link to log directly into their field portal.

## Core field app routes
- /field: Primary mobile dashboard displaying today’s assigned jobs and active clock-in status.
- /field/login: Passwordless magic link authentication portal for crew members.
- /field/choose: Multi-job selector when a technician is assigned multiple sites in a day.
- /field/pay: Summary of captured shift hours and approved timecard entries.
- /field/offline: Offline sync manager for reviewing and submitting locally cached job notes.



# Done State
Field technicians will receive secure mobile access to their assigned jobs without viewing company financials.

# Common Failure
**Crew member did not receive their invite.**
Check that the technician’s email address is correct and ask them to check spam/junk folders for the magic link.

**Crew member sees financial reports.**
Ensure the user was invited as a Crew Member, not an Owner or Office user.
