# Purpose
Configure milestone installment schedules and understand proportional platform fee allocation.

# Steps
## Structure milestone installments
1. When creating a quote or invoice, select "Milestone / Stage Payments".
2. Add stages (e.g. 33% Deposit on Approval, 33% Rough-in Passed, 34% Final Completion).
3. Trigger each installment invoice as the corresponding job phase is completed.

## Proportional fee allocation across installments
Platform fees are allocated proportionally across each installment relative to the total eligible service subtotal. This ensures that installment splits always sum to the exact fee basis when the invoice is paid in full.



# Done State
You will maintain positive cash flow on large projects with structured progress payments.

# Common Failure
**Customer wants to pay an installment early.**
Open the job file, find the staged invoice item, and click "Send Now".
