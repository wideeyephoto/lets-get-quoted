# Purpose
Design pages, connect custom domains via domains.letsgetquoted.com, and understand subdomain rename risks.

# Steps
## Customize trade content and branding
1. Open Website Builder to select your trade layout, color scheme, logo, and photo gallery.
2. Add your service pages, service area radius, and customer review showcases.
3. Preview your site on desktop, tablet, and mobile viewports.

## Connect your custom domain (e.g. www.yourcompany.com)
- A Record: Point host "@" to IP address 76.76.21.21.
- CNAME Record: Point host "www" to domains.letsgetquoted.com (do not use outdated hostnames).
- Automatic SSL: Free Let’s Encrypt SSL certificates are provisioned automatically once DNS propagation is verified.

## Subdomain renaming warning: No redirect
WARNING: If you use an LGQ subdomain (e.g. yourname.letsgetquoted.com) and rename it after launch, there is NO automatic redirect. All existing QR codes, vehicle wrap links, and search engine index links will immediately break.



# Done State
You will launch a high-converting contractor website that ranks locally and captures qualified leads 24/7.

# Common Failure
**Domain shows DNS verification pending.**
Verify your CNAME points to domains.letsgetquoted.com and A record points to 76.76.21.21. DNS changes can take up to 24-48 hours to propagate.

**Subdomain was renamed and old links fail.**
Update external links, marketing materials, and Google Business Profile URLs to your new subdomain.
