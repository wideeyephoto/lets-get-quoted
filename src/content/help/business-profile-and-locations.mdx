# Purpose
Configure public contact details, brand identity, service areas, and multi-location addresses.

# Steps
## Enter shared business details
1. Go to Settings → Business and enter your public business name, phone, email, and website.
2. Upload a high-resolution company logo for display on customer quotes, invoices, and the portal header.
3. Set your primary operating timezone so automated messages and calendar slots sync accurately.

## Keep legal and physical addresses accurate
Your physical mailing address is used for 10DLC carrier registration and anti-spam footer compliance on marketing emails. Ensure your legal business name matches IRS records.



# Done State
Your estimates, invoices, client portal, and public site will present consistent and accurate company details.

# Common Failure
**Address changes do not appear on past invoices.**
Past invoices preserve historical legal records; newly created invoices and quotes will reflect the updated business profile.
