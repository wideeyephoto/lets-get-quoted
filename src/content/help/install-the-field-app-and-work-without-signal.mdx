# Purpose
Install the field app on mobile home screens, queue updates offline, and sync job notes when reconnected.

# Steps
## Install the Field App to your mobile home screen
1. iOS (Safari): Open your field access link, tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari, and select "Add to Home Screen".
2. Android (Chrome): Open your field access link, tap the 3-dot menu in Chrome, and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
3. Launch the app from your home screen icon for full-screen mobile operation.

## Work in basements and dead zones with offline queueing
- When signal drops, the field app displays an offline status indicator.
- Technicians can continue clocking shifts, checking off punch list items, taking photos, and typing job notes.
- All updates are securely stored in the mobile browser’s encrypted offline storage queue.

## The 12-hour sync window invariant
CRITICAL OFFLINE RULE: As soon as mobile signal or Wi-Fi is restored, open the app or visit /field/offline to sync queued updates. Any offline queue items older than 12 hours will be permanently rejected by the server to avoid overwriting newer changes.



# Done State
Field technicians will log notes, checklists, and timecards even in basements and remote job sites.

# Common Failure
**Offline queue shows pending items after returning to service.**
Open the field app, tap the offline indicator, and click "Sync Now".

**App asks for login again when reopening.**
Ensure you launch the app from the installed Home Screen icon rather than opening private/incognito browser tabs.
