# Purpose
Understand workspace roles, server-enforced access gates, plan allowances, and live feature readiness.

# Steps
## Know the three workspace roles
- Owner: Unrestricted access to the entire business, billing, plans, payouts, team invitations, and irreversible actions.
- Office Staff: Invited via email to access permitted dashboard pages (Leads, Schedule, Messages, Jobs, Reports) as bounded by server security gates. Note: there are no individual capability checkboxes in the UI.
- Crew: Field workers who access assigned jobs, timecards, arrival buttons, and in-app voice notes via their magic link field portal (/field). They do not access the back-office dashboard.

## Separate four kinds of feature readiness
1. Role gate: Does the user role (Owner vs Office vs Crew) have server clearance for the requested action?
2. Plan entitlement: Does your active subscription tier include sufficient seats, text credits, or storage?
3. Operational prerequisites: Are external connections (such as Stripe Connect or carrier 10DLC registration) complete?
4. Status vs Live: An on/off switch in settings expresses intent, but the feature only works live when dependencies are satisfied.



# Done State
You will understand what owners, office staff, and field crew can access without confusing plan limits or server gates.

# Common Failure
**An office user cannot access certain actions like sending quotes or merging clients.**
High-trust financial and destructive actions require Owner role clearance.

**A feature is enabled but not sending messages.**
Verify carrier registration under Settings → Business and check that text credits are available.
