# Purpose
Invite office teammates by email, manage secure sign-in methods, and remove access cleanly.

# Steps
## Invite an office team member by email
1. Go to Settings → Team and click "Invite Office User".
2. Enter the person’s email address ({ email }). Note: There is no capability picker in the UI; office users receive standard office dashboard access.
3. Send the invitation. The user receives an email link to set up their password and log in.
4. Office users can access Leads, Schedule, Messages, and Jobs, while owner-only actions (payouts, billing, plan changes) remain restricted.

## Remove access cleanly without deleting history
When a team member leaves, remove their membership from Settings → Team. Historical audit logs, messages, and quote entries created by that user remain intact.



# Done State
Your office team will collaborate securely with role-gated access to dashboard operations.

# Common Failure
**Office invite fails to send.**
Check your plan allowance to verify available office seats, and ensure the email is not already an active user.

**Office user cannot open billing or payouts.**
Billing and payout settings are strictly restricted to the workspace Owner.
