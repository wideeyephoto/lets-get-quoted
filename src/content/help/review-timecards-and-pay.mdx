# Purpose
Audit shift punches, resolve open shift anomalies, approve pay periods, and export payroll CSVs.

# Steps
## Audit and approve crew timecards
1. Open Timecards to review clocked hours grouped by employee, date, and assigned job.
2. Inspect flagged anomalies (e.g. shifts left open overnight or missing lunch breaks).
3. Edit punch times with a documented reason if manual corrections are required.
4. Click "Approve Pay Period" to lock approved hours against further editing.

## Payroll CSV export details
Click "Export Payroll CSV" to generate a payroll file formatted for QuickBooks, Gusto, or ADP. NOTE: The export includes all hourly shift punches and deliberately excludes salaried workers, who do not log hourly timecards.



# Done State
You will run accurate job costing and export clean payroll records with verified audit trails.

# Common Failure
**A salaried employee is missing from the payroll CSV.**
The payroll CSV export is designed for hourly punch workers. Salaried compensation is tracked in general accounting.

**Technician forgot to clock out.**
Click the open shift row, enter the correct end time, and save the audit reason.
