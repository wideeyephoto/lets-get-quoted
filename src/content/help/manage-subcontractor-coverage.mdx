# Purpose
Track subcontractor 1099 agreements, Certificate of Insurance (COI) expirations, and trade assignments.

# Steps
## Track subcontractor credentials and COIs
1. Add trade partners and 1099 subcontractors in Crew → Subcontractors.
2. Upload Certificate of Insurance (COI) PDFs and record policy expiration dates.
3. Set automated alerts 30 days before policy expiration.

## Assign sub trades to project milestones
Assign subcontractors to specialized job phases (e.g. electrical rough-in, roofing) with clear scope instructions and site access dates.



# Done State
You will protect your business from liability by ensuring all subcontractors have active insurance on file.

# Common Failure
**Subcontractor shows an expired COI warning banner.**
Request an updated insurance certificate and update the policy expiration date in their profile.
