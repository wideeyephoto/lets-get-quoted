# Purpose
Route urgent new leads, customer replies, and field updates directly to your mobile phone.

# Steps
## Configure Owner Alert Phone
1. Go to Settings → Business and enter your personal mobile phone number in "Owner Alert Phone".
2. Verify the number with the 6-digit confirmation code sent to your phone.
3. Select the specific alert triggers you want forwarded via SMS.

## Choose your alert trigger events
- New Incoming Leads: Immediate alert when a homeowner submits a web intake form or texts your number.
- Quote Approved & Deposit Paid: Instant notification when a client signs an estimate and pays a deposit.
- Customer Message Replies: Receive direct notifications when an active client texts a question.
- Emergency Service Requests: Priority routing for after-hours urgent repair inquiries.



# Done State
You will receive immediate SMS notifications for critical business events without constantly watching the dashboard.

# Common Failure
**Alert texts are not arriving on mobile.**
Verify your phone number in Settings → Business and confirm that notification toggles are switched on.
