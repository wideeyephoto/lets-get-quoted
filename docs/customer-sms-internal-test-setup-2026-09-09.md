## Prepared scope

Only controlled, explicitly enrolled test handsets may receive simulated
booking-request, missed-call/post-call and reply messages. Messages must identify
Let's Get Quoted and clearly say they are tests. No actual homeowner or unrelated
contractor customer is included. Runtime test branding and the actual enrollment
surface still need verification after the provider confirms this arrangement.

Proposed examples for review, not already-approved or already-sent messages:

- “Let's Get Quoted TEST: We received your simulated appointment request. This
  is not a confirmed appointment. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.”
- “Let's Get Quoted TEST: We missed your test call. Reply with a time for the
  internal team to follow up. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.”
- “Let's Get Quoted TEST: Your test note was saved. Reply STOP to opt out or
  HELP for help.” Use the last example only for a confirmed saved outcome.

## Registration wording correction

The generic contractor template described enrollment methods not established
by the inspected quote form and incorrectly suggested STOP cancels appointments.
It now describes the form's contact-preference selection and request-specific
disclosure, requires verification of actual enrollment evidence, and separates
SMS opt-out from appointment changes. The template is proposed application copy;
it does not establish carrier approval. It is also distinct from the internal
LGQ test campaign above.
